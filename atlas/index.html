About this prototype

These are modern boundaries, including Alaska and Hawaiʻi insets. Colored regions and marker dates are synthetic demonstrations, not historical claims or a map of slavery. The timeline begins in 1619; this is not the beginning of African presence throughout the Americas.

Boundaries: U.S. Atlas / U.S. Census Bureau (ISC). Elevation: Mapzen terrain tiles, USGS 3DEP / GMTED2010 / SRTM and NOAA ETOPO1. Rivers, lakes and neighbouring coastlines: Natural Earth (public domain). Relief has been downsampled and visually exaggerated, and the land colouring is an illustrative palette drawn from elevation, slope and position — not a land-cover survey.
