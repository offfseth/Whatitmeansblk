Sources & method

Follow the evidence.

What supports each entry

The references beneath each milestone support its date, summary, and context. Source labels distinguish original records from institutional histories and scholarly interpretation. Review dates record when the sources were consulted, not when an event happened.

Long movements have approximate date ranges. Period filters include events that overlap a chapter, so the Great Migration can appear in several periods. Map markers connect milestones and city stories to documented places and time ranges. National and regional entries without a city pin remain accessible on the map. A local connection can cover a shorter period than the whole movement.

How this collection was written

New entries use original summaries. This collection reproduces no source photographs or direct quotations. Links let you read the underlying evidence at its publisher.
